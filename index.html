About

Data scientist building customer value and loyalty models for El Corte Inglés, through WhiteboxML.

I studied Computer Engineering and got into data science out of curiosity about machine learning and neural networks, back before LLMs were everywhere. When they came along I was just as curious about them, and went deep on how they actually work too.

The business side of the job has become more and more interesting to me. Understanding how our predictions are actually used changes what I build and how I measure it, and it's where some of my best improvements have come from.

AI agents are part of every stage of how I work: research, weighing up design decisions, implementation and analysis. I keep up with new models, papers and how the best engineers use these tools, keep refining my own workflow, and share what's worth it with my team. Through all of it, I keep judgement, technical criteria, taste and the final call in my own hands, since I believe that's what still sets us apart.

More than anything, I love learning. Outside of AI I'm just as happy going down a rabbit hole on macroeconomics, new tech and gadgets, psychology, or whatever else has caught my curiosity at the moment.

Away from work I'm hopelessly addicted to padel, I love scuba diving, I can't go long without music, and I'm slightly obsessed with typing (100+ WPM average).

I'm fluent in Spanish and English. I've always enjoyed learning languages, so I also know some basic Norwegian, a little German and a touch of Portuguese.

Customer Lifetime Value Nov 2025 – Nov 2026

A production CLV system whose predictions feed a monthly process that flags spending dips in loyal customers so the business can re-engage them.

  • Built end-to-end LightGBM pipelines: rolling-window features over customer and sales history, an RFM framework and careful leakage control, for annual and quarterly spend split into per-category models.
  • Diagnosed a long-standing training failure (autoscaling evicting nodes mid-job, which distributed LightGBM couldn't tolerate), unlocking training on the full dataset of tens of millions of rows and a clear lift in metrics.
  • Co-led the migration from Databricks and Azure Data Lake to Snowflake, rebuilding the codebase into a typed, node-based ETL framework with faster runtimes.
  • Built dedicated models for low-frequency customers: sparse-aware features plus signals from similar customers, found via approximate nearest-neighbour search (FAISS) over customer embeddings.
  • Added new signal sources (GA4 web analytics, promotion behaviour) and broadened evaluation beyond error metrics with ranking measures (Spearman, capture@K) the business could read directly.
